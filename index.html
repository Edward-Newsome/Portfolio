PROJECT 01 / DETAIL

Adaptive Origami Façade

Objective
Develop an adaptive façade capable of responding to solar conditions while supporting natural ventilation and retrofit installation.

Engineering approach
The selected solution uses simplified kaleidocycle geometry. Individual mechanisms are grouped into a larger hexagonal module, supported by an external exoskeleton so the system can be installed independently of the existing curtain wall.

Controls & prototype
An ESP32-based control system interfaces with servo motors through a PCA9685 driver. Solar intensity and sun position are sensed using photodiodes/LDRs. The physical prototype uses laser-cut acrylic panels and 3D-printed connectors.

PROJECT 02 / DETAIL

Woodchip Briquette Maker

Objective
Design a continuous pneumatic machine capable of producing consistent 70 × 70 mm woodchip briquettes.

My role
As team lead, I coordinated a six-person engineering team through feasibility, design decisions and development of the proposed machine.

Engineering approach
The design incorporated pneumatic compression, a welded S275 frame and requirements around continuous operation, cost, safety and manufacturability.
